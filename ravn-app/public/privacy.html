Privacy Policy

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Ravn is built on a local-first philosophy with zero analytics tracking, zero behavioral tracking, and strict local cryptographic licensing.

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1 Our Core Privacy Philosophy

Ravn Download Manager ("Ravn", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. Unlike legacy download accelerators or ad-supported utilities, Ravn is built with an absolute local-first, zero-tracking architecture.

🛡️ Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest user data, browsing histories, or downloaded content under any circumstance.

2 Data We Do NOT Collect

When using the Ravn macOS Desktop Application or Browser Extensions, we never record, collect, or transmit:

  • Downloaded File Contents: The files, videos, music, archives, or ISOs you download remain 100% private on your local disk.
  • Browsing History & URLs: We do not maintain any logs or telemetry of the websites you visit or the URLs you add to your download queue.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords, session cookies, or authorization tokens used for downloads remain strictly within your local machine and browser.
  • Third-Party Analytics Telemetry: No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or telemetry SDKs are embedded inside the macOS application.

3 Data We Collect & Process

We only collect and process minimal data necessary to fulfill purchases and validate cryptographic licenses:

  • Email Address: Provided during checkout or 7-day trial registration to deliver your cryptographic license key, purchase invoice, and customer support notifications.
  • Hardware UUID Hash: A non-reversible cryptographic hash of your Mac's hardware identifier (IOPlatformUUID) is transmitted solely during initial license activation or 7-day trial validation to prevent unauthorized license duplication. This identifier cannot be used to trace your physical location or personal identity.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Payments are processed directly by Stripe, Inc. (a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor). We never view or store full credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank details on our servers.

4 Browser Extensions (Chrome & Safari)

The Ravn companion browser extension operates under a strict Single Purpose Policy:

  • Local Proxy Communication: The extension detects download links or media streams (.m3u8, .mpd) and transmits them directly to your locally running Ravn desktop application via local loopback (http://127.0.0.1:6543).
  • No Cloud Routing: No URLs, cookies, or video stream metadata captured by the extension are ever routed through our remote web servers.
  • Permission Declarations: Permissions requested (e.g. contextMenus, downloads, cookies) are utilized solely to intercept file requests and transfer them to your local download client with authentic headers intact.

5 Security & Cryptography

Ravn utilizes Ed25519 asymmetric cryptography via Apple CryptoKit. Once your license key is issued, all license verification and feature tier checks execute 100% offline on your Mac without requiring persistent server heartbeats.

All communication between the web portal, payment endpoints, and SMTP transactional email servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS protocols.

6 Data Retention & User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy regulations (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation and California Consumer Privacy Act), you retain full rights over your data:

  • Right to Access: You may retrieve your active licenses at any time through our License Portal using your registered email.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request complete deletion of your customer record and email from our database by contacting support.
