Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about licensing, platform compatibility, and features.

Which macOS versions and Macs are supported? +
Ravn is compiled as a Universal 2 binary with native support for all Apple Silicon chips (M1, M1 Pro/Max, M2, M3, M4) and Intel x86_64 processors. Minimum requirement is macOS 14.0 Sonoma or macOS 15.0+ Sequoia.
How does the 7-Day Free Trial work? +
When you choose the Annual plan, you get 7 full days of unrestricted access to all Pro features (32 streams, Media Studio, Spatial VR). You can cancel anytime before the trial ends in your account portal with zero charges.
How does the offline cryptographic license work? +
Ravn uses Ed25519 asymmetric cryptography. When your license is activated, a mathematically signed token is issued that the macOS client verifies locally using Apple's native CryptoKit framework. You can use Ravn completely offline on airplanes or without internet.
How do I capture downloads from Chrome & Safari? +
Ravn includes native browser integration extensions for Safari, Google Chrome, Brave, Arc, and Firefox that automatically route downloads directly to Ravn with 1 click.
